## Two Column Tie
- the idea is to tie 2 any columns (limbs) together.
- wrap the rope around both columns several times.
- important thing is not to twist the rope for beauty and comfort.
- cross the bite and the tail of the rope and wrap them around the ropes wrapping columns between the columns themselves.
- tight the rope and make a knot.
- make sure there is free space between the ropes wrapping the columns and the columns themselves to not overtight columns, but also make sure ropes are not too loose.
